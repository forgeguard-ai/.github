# Support

ForgeGuard AI is an independent lab, not a support organization; response times vary.

- **Documentation:** each project's documentation is on [forgeguard.ai](https://forgeguard.ai)
  and in the repository's `docs/` directory.
- **Bugs and feature requests:** open an issue on the relevant repository, with versions,
  hardware, and reproduction steps.
- **Security problems:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not open a public issue.
- **Anything else:** contact@forgeguard.ai.
